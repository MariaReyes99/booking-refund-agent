# SKILL: Escalation playbook
- Legal language (lawyer, Disputes Tribunal, Consumer Guarantees Act, claim, court): escalate to legal. Do not admit liability, do not offer compensation, acknowledge and give a timeframe.
- Account changes (email, payment method, closure, deletion): accounts team after identity verification. The agent never makes these changes.
- Two-factor judgment for legal: counter-model risk score + a person from legal decides.
